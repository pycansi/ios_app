App Name

T-Trading Assistant

Introduction

Broadly speaking, from opening a position to closing a position is a collection of T trades. This app is used to make T-trading plans and record related trades.

App Description

- Privacy-first, fully local app, with worry-free data security

- Create buy orders or sell orders for stocks; you can fill in only the planned price to remind yourself. After the actual trade, fill in the actual trade price. Under each buy or sell order, you can create the opposite trade. After closing, the profit or loss of this T trade will be displayed. The sum of the profit or loss of each T trade is the profit or loss of that stock.
