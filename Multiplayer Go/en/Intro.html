Application Name

Multiplayer Go

Introduction

A Multiplayer Chess Battle, a different Go experience.

App Features

- In addition to the standard 19-line board, it also supports various boards ranging from as few as 5 lines to as many as 25 lines

- Supports up to 8 players in the same game, with selectable human or computer players

- Standalone app, no internet connection required

- No ads, no in-app purchases
