App Name

Stickman Parkour & Build

Introduction

Satisfying combos + endless levels

App Description

- Silky-smooth controls. The left half of the screen is a movement joystick; swipe on the right half to perform actions, and actions can be chained into combos.

- Build levels freely. All built-in levels are handcrafted by the author. Edit built-in levels to build your own levels based on them.

- Levels can be imported and exported via JSON text. Want your friends to challenge a level you just made? Just send them the level's JSON text.

- No ads, no in-app purchases
